Welcome to Arya Bhavan

(a prominent international chain with branches in Chennai, Coimbatore, the UK, and Paris)


Arya Bhavan is a widely recognized chain of pure-vegetarian South Indian restaurants, highly popular across Chennai and beyond. It is beloved by locals and travelers alike for serving fresh, authentic, and comforting vegetarian meals—ranging from crispy dosas and fluffy idlis to classic South Indian filter coffee.

Core Highlights

Signature Menu Items: Renowned for their crispy Paper Roast Dosas, fluffy Idli-Vada combos, rich South Indian Thalis, and excellent morning filter coffee.

Cuisine Variety: While specializing in traditional South Indian, many branches also serve North Indian, Chinese, and quick street-food snacks

Vibe & Service: Known for its clean, family-friendly environment. It offers quick service and is a staple stop for breakfast, lunch, or dinner

Value: Highly appreciated for offering premium-tasting, homemade-style food at highly competitive and pocket-friendly prices

Locations in Chennai

Because it operates as a widespread regional network rather than a single location, there are multiple branches serving the greater Chennai and Tamil Nadu area:

Kolathur: Situated on 100 Feet Road, it is a well-visited spot in North Chennai.

West Mambalam: Located near SRM Hospital, serving as a reliable daily neighborhood eatery.

Anna Nagar / Egmore: Frequently found in various commercial and residential hubs, offering convenient and accessible vegetarian dining.

Highway Routes: Arya Bhavan outlets are also popular highway stopovers (such as on the GST Road) offering tired travelers a consistent and hygienic dining experience.
